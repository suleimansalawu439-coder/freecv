# Cvyon Job Matcher Chrome Extension

This extension allows you to instantly extract job descriptions from LinkedIn and Indeed and score your Cvyon resume against them.

## Local Installation (For Testing)

1. Open Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions/`
2. Turn on **Developer mode** (toggle in the top right corner).
3. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top left.
4. Select this `chrome-extension` folder.
5. Pin the Cvyon extension to your browser toolbar.

## Usage

1. Navigate to any job posting on **LinkedIn** or **Indeed**.
2. Click the Cvyon extension icon in your toolbar.
3. Click **Score My Match**.
4. You will be redirected to the Cvyon Builder with the job description pre-filled in the ATS Grader!

## Publishing to the Chrome Web Store

To make this extension available for all users globally, follow these steps:

1. **Zip the Extension:** Compress the entire `chrome-extension` folder into a single `.zip` file.
2. **Create a Developer Account:** Go to the [Chrome Web Store Developer Dashboard](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole/) and sign in with your Google account.
3. **Pay Registration Fee:** Pay the one-time $5 developer registration fee.
4. **Upload:** Click **New Item** and upload your `.zip` file.
5. **Fill out details:** Provide a Title, Description, Icon (128x128), and Promotional images.
6. **Privacy Policy:** Ensure you link to Cvyon's Privacy Policy and declare that you only scrape job description text (no PII).
7. **Submit:** Click **Submit for Review**. Google usually approves extensions within 1-3 business days.
